# My agent: Smart Recipe & Kitchen Concierge
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps home cooks find, customize, and cook delicious meals with a catalog of recipes, pantry ingredients, and culinary techniques.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User dietary restrictions (allergies, vegan, keto), family size, disliked ingredients, favorite cuisines, and available pantry items across sessions
- Tools: Search recipes by ingredients/dietary tags, add/update pantry inventory, scale ingredient measurements, calculate cooking times
- Catalog/UI: Recipe cards (dish title, prep time, difficulty, ingredient checklist), pantry items table, step-by-step cooking cards
- Image gen: AI-generated photos of finished dishes and presentation styling
- Sandbox: Code sandbox for recipe scaling calculations, unit conversions (e.g. metric to imperial), and macro/nutrition estimation

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for recipe scaling & nutritional calculations, grocery shopping list export
